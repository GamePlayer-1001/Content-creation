My friend spent 3 hours tweaking prompts in ChatGPT to nail one post.

Six versions. Same content. All different formatting.

Turns out he wasn't writing. He was doing layout work in a dialog box.

Here's the deal—most people think "AI writing" means pumping out 10 versions and picking the best one.

It doesn't.

Real workflow? One-directional:

1. Write a 300-word structure note (no AI)
2. Feed it to AI once
3. Polish the output

That's it. One generation.

The time sink isn't generation—it's decision fatigue from comparing versions.

Your best output gets destroyed by the exact process meant to create it.

**New rule: don't open AI until you've written a structure note.**
